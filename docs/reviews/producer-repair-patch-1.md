## Interpretation

Engine events contain channel ID, schedule item key, child PID, wall and engine-relative monotonic milliseconds. Segment names include item and generation identity; an observer's PID/item identify the observing producer, not necessarily the segment's owner because an existing playlist may contain older entries. Segment `modified_ms` is a filesystem last-write timestamp, an approximation of encode completion, and `wall_ms` is when a 100 ms poll first saw the playlist contents, not the atomic replacement instant. For 30 observations matching the observer's item in this capture, observed wall time minus mtime was 1–97 ms; this is not a general publication latency guarantee. Clock adjustment, mtime resolution, polling and initial observations of existing segments affect comparisons. The capture records EXTINF, PDT and paired wall/monotonic timestamps; TS byte receipt remains an upper bound and its clock pairing uncertainty is retained. No input URLs or credentials are added to these telemetry events.
