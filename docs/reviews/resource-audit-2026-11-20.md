**Findings and actions.**

1. **Medium — expensive producer retention is intentional but materially affects capacity.** Browser disconnected around 14:40:47. Non-prewarmed FFmpeg processes exited at 14:41:58, 14:42:00 and 14:42:53, consistent with the 120-second last-access timeout in `engine.rs`; channel 1 remained because prewarm is enabled. Four simultaneous producers after a single viewer's sequential switches are expected during this grace period. No encoder leak or exhaustion was established in this short test; test longer, more viewers and admission utilization before sizing capacity. Expose per-channel active/idle/last-access and encoder admission metrics, and consider a configurable grace period after evaluating warm-switch tradeoffs.
2. **Medium — cold-start 503s are preparing responses, not proven resource exhaustion.** At 14:38:53 channel 4 requested a manifest; server resolver finished successfully after 921 ms, but the browser received four one-second 503 responses before switching away; playout was ready by 14:39:12 and warm playback succeeded. `playlist_for_channel` wraps readiness in a one-second timeout and maps failures to 503; readiness itself waits up to 45 seconds. This explains the HTTP behavior, but does not identify which startup stage dominated or prove the 16-encoder cap was hit. Instrument source resolution, admission wait, first encoded segment, readiness watermark and response reason separately; report preparing distinctly in client telemetry.
3. **Medium — investigate external I/O and fallback with direct metrics.** At 14:39:16 channel 4's next-source resolver returned unresolved after 2,033 ms, then succeeded in 913 ms at 14:39:17. Channel 3's retained playout history contained `source_unavailable` slate, while its latest segments were real media. Transient D-state samples are compatible with I/O waits but neither establish NAS root cause nor prove it caused a specific 503. Add bounded per-source cache-hit, metadata/read latency and fallback counters and correlate with NAS-side telemetry. Per-segment ffprobe is bounded by source code at five seconds and 8 MiB; the sampling saw it but cannot count all invocations or establish its total cost. Instrument durations/counts before optimizing.
4. **Low — host swap nearly full before the test.** Available RAM remained ample and swap free was stable, so this run does not establish swap pressure as the cause of startup latency. Monitor host swap-in/out and memory pressure over a longer interval.
